# Ikuya Mitty - Whales Notes
With some comps I have found that certain pain points with Ikuya Mitt plan. Most of these can be alleviated with safety healing.
However with some understanding of the fight timelines and specific comps, there a small changes that can be made, which add safety and consistency.
Use the [timeline](/mitPlan/timeline.md) in combination with these notes to get a better understanding of the healing profile of the fight.

Each Note will be structured as such:

Mechanic Name
- Relevant Timestamps

Suggestions
- Such as specific timings
- And/or changes

More in detail explanation
- Gives reasoning of why I suggest any change

## Phase 1

### Mystery Magic 1

Time: 00:25 - 01:00

#### Suggestions

If you comp has an AST:

- In comps with 1 shield tank (pld/war) and one mit tank (drk/gnb) ignore role based times (i.e mt/ot)
    instead always have the shield tank use their mit during Graven Image 1(GI) cast and the mit tank use their 90s mit after the fire spread/stack
- Ast when using NS use Asp. Helios + Sunsign after the fire stack/spread
- Sch Constellation 1 before Wave Cannon/Towers, Second Constellation after Confetti KB (00:5x). Ensure Fairy is centered immediately after it returns from dissapation
- Sch deploy off D4 
- Drks can TBN a squishy after the fire stack/spread and have it back up for tb (D4 unless sch is deploying a spreadlo of them, in which case d3)
    needs to timed at around 00:40s ish (2s after stack/spread). This is pretty tight timing
- d3 can 90s mit right at end of GI cast, This will set up for a better usage later on, but results in towers not having samba. This is generally livable but something to be aware of.

#### Justifications

The mystery magic sequence is the probably the biggest pain point of the phase, and the choices made with mitagation have flow on consequences to the next biggest pain point,
which is Graven Image 2 (or specifically the Confetti 2 into Gravity Soaks). Most of the suggestions I make are aimed at shifting timings to keep the safety of the mit plan whilst adding
additional safety to GI 2.

The issue arises from the damage from the confetti kb. With the current plan the damage from Gravity is livable from full hp, however the KB from confetti when pre-shielded still does 70k resulting in a tight window for requiring in top ups to avoid dying to the combined soak damage. In WHM/x comps this is not an issue as Liturgy of a bell will provide this healing automatically (though you will still probably need either a rapture/ixochole/indom in additon). 

##### Swapping to job based timing vs role based timing for Tank 90s

If you follow the suggestion for the tank 90s, this will allow for either a shake/divine Veil as you get knocked back from confetti, utilizing both the raw heal and the shield for the gravity soaks. The shield portion provides roughly the same over mitigation value as having DRK/GNB while providing additional value from the raw heal. Additionally its just easier to time compared to utilizing the mit tanks 90s in a similar fashion

##### AST NS timings

Due to Lightspeed, it's fairly trivial for AST go center after stack/spread and move back to their Wave Cannon position. Utilizing the Asp. Helios in this fashion gives you the full value of the gcd and is adds additional safety to wave cannons/tower soaks.

##### Sch Constellation timings

If you have people dying to kb, using constellation 2 there should fix it. Light of Judgement is generally livable with just succor but mileage mit vary

##### Deploying off D4

D4s are the players most likely to miss a heal so giving them an additonal 70k of shields is pretty good at solving this, yell at Blm/Smn and Pct's to use their personal shield here too.

##### TBN the d4/d3

Same as above D3 and D4 can sometimes miss heals/mits right before an instance of damage that does 370k +/-5%. I feel like this might be rather strict timing however. Fire Spread/Stack resolves at 00:38, Wave cannon at 00:42 and towers at 00:46. So it should be possible to reliably TBN after the spread/stack and have it last long enough either way. Also pay attention to the mp requirements as you need another 3k mp for the tb

##### D3 90s timing

If you want to be a gigachad, it is possible to use your 90s mit immediately after the GI1 cast, have it cover the spread/stacks and wave cannon. This then allows you to use it again as being Knocked back by confetti 2, to cover the Gravity Soaks whilst still lasting long enough for Light of Judgement (just). The downside is that it won't cover the tower soaks. Consider doing this if you have issues surviving GI2 gravity soaks.

### Graven Image 2

Time: 01:16 - 02:13

#### Suggestions

- If doing 8 man puddle drops, ast can move Macrocosmos to second set of tethers (use it right before the puddle tethers resolve, appox. 01:44 - 01:45)
    this will naturally expire as confetti 2 knock resolves, ensuring that the party is full hp going into gravity soaks
- Send tank 90s and d3 90s as you get knocked back bu confetti 2 (ot 90s in mitplan, shield tank 90s if you follow my previous suggestions)
- PLD Passage of Arms the light of judgement
- If you have a PLD, consider sending Plenary/CU as being KB, strictly speaking its livable without passage and the h1 10% but if something else is missed you are probably dead
- Both tanks take a shitton of autos damage here, ensure the tank holding aggro in first set has some assistance but save more resources for whoever is holding aggro in second set.
- If tank isn't invulning second Hyperdrive ensure healers give the tank some love
- Sch can drop soil right before kb and have it last for Light of Judgement

#### Justifications

Pretty much the same reasons provided for Mystery Magic 1, generally just making the healers jobs a little easier.

##### Macrocosmos Timing

This wasn't possible with old strat for puddle soaks as the other light party would be out of range with this specific timing. Not sure if its actually better or not but probably adds a bit more consistency.

##### 90s tank/d3 mits

Its possible to cover both the gravity soaks + light of judgement with 90s mits which makes the gravity soaks more consistent. 15s mits will last long enough to cover the light of judgement.
It's fairly safe to cut shields tanks mit from light of judgement (essentially its shifting the healing required before gravity soaks, which is a 2s window, to afterwards wheres theres about 10s before Light of Judgement)

##### Pld Passage of Arms

Use either here, or on the first light of judgement, but using here is preferable imo

##### Shifting healer 10%'s

Again just adds extra safety to gravity soaks, the Light of Judgement is livable with just shield healer gcd, a 10%, a 5% and a 15% (d3 mit). Cutting the h1 10% here will result in the party getting quite low, however there is a lot of time to heal up before the next instance of damage (rough 35s)

##### Soil Timing

Soil's broken, it last longer the Kerachole due to jank. Drop it just before KB and it will last for Light of Judgement

### Graven Image 3 - Aka Arrows

- SCH can squeeze in a spreadlo here, but need to deploy it no later then 02:22 to ensure its back in time for Forsaken.
- Ast NS if a sch spreadlos as above, Asp. Helios After knockback (will need either lightspeed or swift) otherwise Asp. Helios before the knockback
- 15s mits (sunsign, soil, kera, missionary, HoL) roughly 2s after being knocked back to cover both Idulgent Will + the spread/stack afterwards

#### Justifications

The spreadlo is pretty free and reduces the damage taken from kb significantly, however the timing is rather tight as you can't do it too early (will expire before the kb) or too late (won't be up in time for forsaken).

##### Ast N/s timing

You get strictly more value from doing the Asp. Helios afterwards but is harder to pull of without a charge of lightspeed. Regardless of what you do ensure that you throw out some healing afterwards so that the party lives through the Indulgent Will

##### 15s mits

This is the timing required to have it cover both instances of damage, however you could just mit the Confetti knockbacks and Indulgent Will and be fine as long as healers top party up as the stack/spread is livable without too much mit
